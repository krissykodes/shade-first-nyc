Shade Queue NYC

Which empty street-tree pits should NYC Parks plant first to shade the most sidewalk?

Parks' Neighborhood Tree Planting Program takes neighborhoods in order of the Health Department's Heat Vulnerability Index, HVI 5 first. HVI is scored per ZIP, so every pit in a ZIP gets the same priority.

Mapped empty street pits by ZIP HVI

Example: South Jamaica (11434) and Queens Village (11429) are both HVI 5. One is 9% vacant, the other 35%. HVI can't separate them, and it says nothing about which pit to plant.

    Reading the numbers
    Shade yield
    How much of a new tree's afternoon shadow would fall on sidewalk that is sunny today. 1.0 means all of it; 0 means it would land on the road, a yard, or a spot a building or tree already shades.
    Sq ft shaded
    The patch of sunny sidewalk the tree keeps in shade, on average, from 2 to 4 pm on a mid-July day. A parking space is about 160 sq ft (9 × 18 ft).
    Tree age
    Years after planting. Size comes from measured growth of Queens street trees (USDA Forest Service).

    Method and data
    • Pits: NYC Parks Forestry Planting Spaces (82zj-84is), psstatus = Empty, pssite = Street, pulled Nov 14 2026.
    • Heat: DOHMH Heat Vulnerability Index by ZCTA (4mhf-duep), used as published. No demographic data enters the pit score.
    • Sun: NOAA solar position for July 15, 2–4 pm EDT, every 30 minutes. The sun moves from southwest and high (67° up) toward the west (48° up).
    • Tree size by years after planting: USDA Forest Service Urban Tree Database (RDS-2016-0005), growth equations for 21 street-tree species measured in Queens, median across species.
    • The tree's shadow is checked at 17 spots per time step. A spot counts if it is on sidewalk (52n9-sdep), not in a building's shadow (Building Footprints 5zhs-2jue, roof heights) and not in an existing street tree's shadow (Forestry Tree Points hn5i-inap, sized from trunk diameter).
    • Checks: –
    • Limits: flat ground, one typical species, no awnings or scaffolding. Pit viability inspections are not yet joined. 191 W 116th St is placed approximately, from neighbouring addresses.
